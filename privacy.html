Privacy

Last updated October 21, 2026

This policy covers theranchsteamlab.com only. Each of our products (Ripple Pool, Snakedoku and Hunchwell) has its own privacy policy on its own site.

What this site collects

Nothing about you personally. This site has no accounts, no forms, no comments and no newsletter.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies.

Analytics

This site does not currently run any analytics. If we add them later, we will use a cookie-free, privacy-friendly service that counts visits and link clicks without identifying you, and we will update this page first.

Things this site loads from elsewhere

  • Fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests them from Google's servers, which see your IP address as part of that request.
  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, to run and secure the site.

Links to our products

Links to our products include a utm_source=ranchsteamlab tag so those sites can tell visitors came from here. Once you leave this site, the privacy policy of the site you visit applies.
